# Virtual Simulation Video Storyboards

The generated videos are silent, captioned, non-clinical walkthroughs. They use
only synthetic phantoms and system diagrams.

## Video 1 — Virtual Surgical Simulation

1. Planning-only disclaimer.
2. Full modular platform overview.
3. Locked configuration and independent safety controller.
4. Room layout and clear bedside/conversion path.
5. Docking one arm at a time to a synthetic phantom.
6. S2 surgeon-controlled motion inside a safe workspace.
7. Stale-camera fault injection and automatic safe hold.
8. Log/replay review and research-only close.

## Video 2 — Virtual Procedure Walkthrough

1. Case/team/configuration verification.
2. Readiness checks and emergency controls.
3. Training drape, sterile adapter, and instrument identity.
4. Camera-first docking and low-speed clearance.
5. Master enable, clutch, scaling, and tool coordination.
6. One-at-a-time instrument exchange.
7. Stop, manual-release readiness, and end-run decision.
8. Undock, component count, log seal, and debrief.

## Narration safety rule

Narration must explain robot state, controls, faults, and team coordination only.
It must not instruct an operator how to cut, dissect, cauterize, suture, drill,
implant, treat anatomy, or manage a clinical complication.
